# References and reproduction scope

## Primary baselines (implemented independently from equations)

1. **Luo et al. (2021)**, *A multi-scale image watermarking based on integer wavelet transform and singular value decomposition*, Expert Systems with Applications, article 114272. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eswa.2020.114272. Supplied `SVD_luo2021.pdf`, Sections 3.1–3.4, Eqs. (6)–(16), (21). `baselines.py`: all four IWT bands, top-left singular-matrix partition, second SVD, inverse SVD, inverse IWT, SHA-1 XOR over **all four** pairs of auxiliary matrices, 8-bit OAM using two-level DWT and 4x4 high-standard-deviation blocks; literal signed extraction. The paper does not fully specify wavelet filter, threshold T, serialization, B, or how the selected positions are conveyed. Our reproducible choices are integer Haar lifting, orthonormal Haar for OAM, T=.03, little-endian float64 row-major serialization, first byte of B=167, stable standard-deviation re-ranking at extraction. T is an implementation parameter, not claimed as a published value. The 28 alpha pairs from Sec. 3.4 are evaluated on training hosts only, using the common seven-condition calibration subset and the paper's objective, rather than claiming exact repetition of its original TDOM attacks. Eqs. (14)–(16) are sign-sensitive and internally inconsistent; literal failures are retained, not silently fixed. The corrected DOCX OAM control is an explicitly separate adaptation, not a paper baseline. NC is calculated even on authentication failure only as a diagnostic; authorized_nc is blank for rejection.

2. **Ganic and Eskicioglu (2005)**, *Robust embedding of visual watermarks using discrete wavelet transform and singular value decomposition*, Journal of Electronic Imaging 14(4), 043004. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1117/1.2137650. Section 2 algorithm and Section 3 parameters: https://citeseerx.ist.psu.edu/document?doi=bf416e1ec5f860b88a6ba01eaec33a5585464a8c&repid=rep1&type=pdf . One-level orthonormal Haar DWT, four SVDs, singular-value addition of watermark singular values, independent recovery of four watermarks using original subband singular values and watermark singular vectors. Published strengths LL=.05 and detail=.005, available in default config. Smaller watermark sizes use the corresponding first N singular values (equivalent to padding the remainder by zeros); this is the explicit multiscale adaptation. Paper uses Pearson correlation of singular values and can use known rotation correction; our common benchmark uses image NC and gives no method the attack angle. The mean of all four recovered NCs is the primary metric, and all four NCs are exported; no ground-truth best-band selection. No authentication mechanism is invented for this baseline.

3. **Liu and Tan (2002)**, *An SVD-based watermarking scheme for protecting rightful ownership*, IEEE Transactions on Multimedia 4(1), 121–128. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/6046.985560. Algorithm also described by Luo et al., Eq. (3) and adjacent discussion. Reference independent implementation: https://github.com/Adam-Rohde/SVD-Based-Watermarking-Schemes/blob/master/Liu%20and%20Tan%202002.py . Full luminance SVD, additive watermark matrix, second SVD, reconstruction with host singular vectors and new singular values, recovery using the stored auxiliary SVD factors and original singular matrix. A smaller watermark is zero-padded in the upper-left corner of the 512x512 singular-value matrix, and the recovered upper-left region is returned. Alpha is calibrated for the common RGB protocol rather than asserted to be the original paper's experimental alpha. No authentication mechanism is added.

## Common protocol adaptations (all methods)

The original papers are grayscale schemes; all are adapted to the same BT.601-style YCbCr luminance interface with chroma retained, 8-bit RGB reconstruction, and the user's two watermark images. Consequently this is an **equation-level reimplementation under a common protocol**, not an exact rerun of original paper datasets, code, numerical libraries or reported tables. Do not describe the new results as published results. The proposed method follows the supplied IWT-Schur-SVD DOCX (Eqs. 1–11), uses real Schur (quasi-upper triangular) and keeps the two SVDs and Arnold permutation. HMAC is a symmetric authentication tag, not a public-key digital signature. BCH, Givens rotation and the fixed synchronization grid are not claimed to be novel primitives.

Makbol & Khoo (2014), DOI source https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1051200414001997, is a relevant future baseline, but its full implementation details were not available during this preparation; it is deliberately not represented by an invented implementation. Likewise, no generic QIM, invented hybrid or modified DOCX is labeled as a published method.
